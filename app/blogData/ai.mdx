# Artificial Intelligence: Overview

## 1. Introduction

AI is a field of computer science dedicated to creating systems that perform tasks requiring human intelligence.

## 2. Core Branches

- **Machine Learning (ML)**: Learning from data.
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Understanding human speech.
- **Computer Vision**: Identifying visual patterns.

## 3. Types of AI

- **Narrow AI (ANI)**: Specific tasks (Siri, Alexa).
- **General AI (AGI)**: Human-level intelligence (Theoretical).

## 4. Modern Applications

- Healthcare (Diagnostics)
- Finance (Fraud detection)
